Before you buy

Is it for the Goethe or the telc exam? +

Both. Chapter 1 explains the differences, and every strategy and model answer notes where the two exams differ. The Playbook is independent and not affiliated with Goethe-Institut or telc.

What level do I need? +

You should have finished A2 or be in a B1 course. If you're a beginner, the Playbook will be too advanced for now.

Is it in English or German? +

Explanations are in simple English, with Urdu notes for tricky grammar. All model answers and phrases are in German, with translations.

How do I get it after paying? +

You get a download link by email right away. The PDF works on phone, tablet and computer, and you can print it.

How is this different from free YouTube videos? +

YouTube gives you scattered tips. The Playbook is one complete, ordered plan for every exam task, with model answers you can study and a 30-day schedule to follow.

Does it include official exam papers? +

No. All tasks and model answers are written by our teachers in the B1 exam style. For official sample papers, see the Goethe and telc websites.

Do I get future updates? +

Yes. When we release a new edition, you get it free by email.

What if it doesn't help me? +

You're covered by our 14-day guarantee. Email us and we'll refund you in full.
