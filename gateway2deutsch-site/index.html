Sound familiar?

B1 isn't hard because of grammar. It's hard because nobody shows you exactly what to fix.

01

You understand, but freeze when you speak

You know the words. Then the examiner asks a question and nothing comes out. Speaking needs real practice with real people, not just apps.

02

Your writing comes back days later, or never

You write a letter, wait a week for corrections, and repeat the same mistakes in the exam. Writing improves with fast, specific feedback.

03

You don't know if you're ready to book the exam

The exam fee is high and your visa, job or university depends on it. Booking too early is expensive. Booking too late costs months.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find your answer? Write to us on WhatsApp or at hello@gateway2deutsch.com.

What's the difference between the Playbook, the Course and Pro? +

The Playbook is a PDF with exam strategies and model answers, best for quick revision. The B1 Course is a complete video course that takes you from A2 to exam-ready. Pro is a subscription for ongoing practice: teacher-checked homework, unlimited mock exams and the speaking club. Many students combine the Course with Pro.

Which exams does this prepare me for? +

Goethe-Zertifikat B1 and telc Deutsch B1. Both test reading, listening, writing and speaking, and our mock exams follow their formats and timing. We are not affiliated with Goethe-Institut or telc.

What level do I need to start? +

You should have finished A2 or be close to it. Not sure? Take the free 15-minute placement test and we'll tell you where to begin.

Is the AI correction reliable? +

The AI marks mistakes fast and explains them, but it can be wrong. That's why on Pro a teacher reviews your homework and writing and confirms or corrects every mark. You always see both.

How do the live speaking sessions work? +

Sessions run online in small groups of up to 8 learners at the same level, led by a teacher. Times are set for Pakistan time and recordings of Q&A sessions are available to members.

How can I pay from Pakistan? +

You can pay in PKR by debit or credit card, JazzCash or Easypaisa. International students can pay in EUR by card.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Cancel Pro from your account settings in two clicks. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

What if it's not for me? +

Every purchase comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. Email us within 14 days and we'll refund you in full.
