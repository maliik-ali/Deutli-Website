FAQ

Questions about the course

Who is this course for? +

Learners who have finished A2 and want to pass Goethe or telc B1, often for a visa, Ausbildung, a job or university in Germany.

How long do I have access? +

For life. You keep every lesson, note and future update.

How much time do I need each week? +

About 4–5 hours a week to finish in 10 weeks. You can go faster or slower; everything is self-paced.

Who checks my homework? +

AI marks it first with explanations. Teacher-reviewed tasks are then checked by a qualified teacher within 48 hours, who confirms or corrects the feedback.

Is there live teaching? +

The lessons are recorded so you can learn anytime. Teacher Plus adds one-to-one speaking mocks and the weekly live speaking club.

Do I get a certificate? +

Yes, a Gateway2Deutsch certificate of completion. It is not an official language certificate; for that you take the Goethe or telc exam.

Can I pay in instalments? +

Yes, Teacher Plus can be paid in 3 monthly instalments in PKR.

What is the refund policy? +

Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Our exam-ready promise applies after you complete all modules and mock exams.
